## Official legal sources / findings

**EU pre-contract information:** [European Commission Your Europe B2C ecommerce](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm) requires a real trader identity, physical/email addresses, telephone when available, delivery restrictions, withdrawal process/exceptions and all applicable taxes/mandatory charges before contract. The Seller Online *partner address* does not by itself identify the product trader or manufacturer. Never substitute a closed showroom or personal data guessed by an AI.

**EU withdrawal and nonconformity:** [EU returns guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm) describes statutory 14-day distance withdrawal, with exceptions such as genuinely made-to-consumer-specifications / clearly personalised goods. Simply beginning production after an order or calling a standard variant "made to order" does **not by itself** prove the exception. [EU legal guarantees](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/guarantees/index_en.htm) preserves remedies for defective/non-conforming items regardless of a voluntary 7-day store policy. Avoid blanket "no cancellation/refund" that suggests overriding mandatory rights. Consumer return costs/notice and market-specific exceptions must be reviewed before paid EU checkout.

**EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR), especially distance offers:** [Consolidated Regulation (EU) 2023/988 Article 19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02023R0988-20260529) and the [Commission guidance](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52025XC06233) require, where applicable, manufacturer name + postal/electronic address, an EU responsible person's name + postal/electronic address if the manufacturer is outside the Union, exact product identifier, and applicable safety/warning details on EU-targeted online offers. The brand owner's residence in Spain does not automatically establish a given product's manufacturer or fulfil the EU responsible-person role. **This is a separate material EU product-offer release gate**, not cured by placing Seller Online LLC's US postal address on Contact. Product-specific scope, language/warnings and operator arrangement require review. Do not invent or publish a responsible person.

**US shipping promises:** [FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule) requires reasonable shipment-date claims and buyer notice/consent/refund when shipment will be delayed. A policy excluding voluntary refunds for changed *events* does not eliminate statutory delayed-shipment rights. Manufacture / dispatch time and post-dispatch carrier transit must be differentiated, and express must not be sold as faster production.

**Privacy/marketing:** Keep the existing controller identity and consent gate distinct from seller/onboarding. Opt-in marketing is separate and unchecked by default; customer email for an order is not marketing consent. Avoid PII in GA4/AI/logs; provide accurate retention/rights notices before collecting customer data. Merchant product/feed review needs consistent seller information, price, currency, shipping and a real completed checkout, unlike Google Ads API OAuth Branding which can proceed independently.

1. Contact / Footer / Terms / Privacy: one consistent role-qualified partner disclosure, exact US operator mailing address, and **no false claim** that online checkout or merchant approval is already active.
2. Shipping: remove outdated "pre-index" and any blanket "worldwide delivery now" claim. Define "estimated delivery" = manufacturing/dispatch + carrier transit (subject to actual route proof). When delay becomes material, use a lawful customer notification/consent/refund process. No payable countries until live carrier route proof.
3. Returns/Terms: preserve 7-day voluntary assistance and product-specific genuine custom exceptions **without** misrepresenting EU withdrawal, nonconforming goods remedies, or FTC shipping delay obligations. Do not imply that a checkbox waives statutory consumer rights.
4. Checkout order summary: exact server merchandise + one cart shipping + €5 for each additional distinct canonical listing + *verified* applicable tax/discount/services. If unknown: `amount_due=null`, **not** fictitious zero tax. Present seller/partner roles, total and policy links before pay; guest checkout without mandatory membership.
5. Product detail EU-targeted offers: GPSR identity, EU responsible person when needed, product identifier/safety/warnings after real manufacturer/responsible-person verification. Product truth and public visual remain frozen otherwise. A customer contact page alone cannot close this gate.
