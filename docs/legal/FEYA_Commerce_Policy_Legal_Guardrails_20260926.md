# FEYA Commerce Policy Legal Guardrails — 26 Sep 2026

Status: implementation guardrail, not legal advice and not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific counsel.

Purpose: preserve the Human Owner's strict anti-free-rental commercial policy without publishing clauses that attempt to waive non-waivable consumer rights.

## Owner commercial intent

- No discretionary order cancellation once an order is placed and production begins.
- No "wear for one event and return" refund model.
- Defect / wrong item / wrong agreed size-color-configuration -> prompt remedy.
- Preferred remedies: adjustment, repair, remake, replacement; exchange/store credit where appropriate.
- Customer should inspect promptly and contact TheFEYA ideally within 1–3 days and within 7 days for the store's voluntary resolution workflow.
- Sale-item voluntary remedies use store credit.
- Made-to-measure, personalized and non-standard custom orders are final sale under store policy except defects/errors and mandatory legal rights.
- Customer event cancellation, weather, photoshoot failure, missed performance or similar outside event is not a discretionary refund reason.
- Delivery estimates are not event-date guarantees.
- Destination import duties/taxes/clearance fees are buyer responsibility where applicable; buyer must cooperate with customs.
- Checkout must require an explicit, unticked acknowledgement of Terms + Returns/Exchange + Shipping, with policy version and timestamp retained.

## Mandatory implementation limits

### 1. Do not say that every made-to-order item automatically has no statutory withdrawal right
For EU-facing sales, the withdrawal exception is strongest for goods made to the customer's specifications or clearly personalized. "Made to order" by itself is not the same thing as "clearly personalized."

Implementation:
- tag true personalized / made-to-measure / non-standard configuration orders explicitly;
- do not rely on the exception for an otherwise standard product merely because it was manufactured after purchase.

Official reference:
https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm

### 2. Do not invent an "intimate item" label as a legal workaround
For EU-facing sales, the hygiene withdrawal exception concerns sealed goods that are not suitable for return for health/hygiene reasons after being unsealed.

Implementation:
- only rely on a hygiene exception where the actual product/packaging workflow supports it;
- if FEYA wants this protection for specific next-to-skin goods, introduce a real tamper-evident hygiene seal and record seal status;
- do not mark ordinary tops/bras automatically non-returnable merely by naming them "intimate."

Official reference:
https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm

### 3. A 7-day notice window is a store workflow, not a waiver of statutory defect rights
The 7-day window may govern FEYA's voluntary fit/exchange workflow and is useful evidence against post-event free-rental behavior, but it must not be written as extinguishing mandatory legal remedies for non-conforming/defective goods.

Official reference:
https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/guarantees/index_en.htm

### 4. Do not use a blanket "carrier loss is never our responsibility" clause
For EU consumer sales, where the seller arranged delivery, risk generally remains with the seller until delivery to the consumer. Carrier/customs delay can be excluded as an event-date guarantee, but lost/damaged parcel remedies must follow applicable law.

Official reference:
https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/guarantees/faq/index_en.htm

### 5. Buyer-paid return shipping must be scoped correctly
For voluntary returns / withdrawal where legally permitted and properly disclosed in advance, buyer return shipping may be chargeable. For defects/non-conformity, mandatory law may require seller-paid remedy costs.

Official reference:
https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm
https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/consumer-contracts-guarantees/consumer-guarantees/index_en.htm

### 6. Public site does not need to explain FEYA's banking/settlement chain
The contracting seller identity/contact information and accepted payment methods/arrangements must be clear where required. The site does not need to narrate internal settlement routing, intermediary commissions or which bank ultimately receives funds unless a payment/provider rule or applicable law specifically requires it.

For EU-facing distance sales, before purchase provide trader identity, geographical address, email and (where available) telephone plus payment/delivery/return information.

Official reference:
https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm

## Chargeback / dispute evidence to persist

For each order:
- order / checkout ID;
- canonical product ID;
- exact variant ID;
- exact configuration_price_id;
- authoritative quote receipt ID and amount/currency;
- selected size/personalization values;
- selected shipping method and presented delivery estimate;
- policy version IDs;
- explicit policy acknowledgement timestamp;
- product-page/version evidence shown at purchase;
- order confirmation email;
- fulfillment/photo/QC evidence where available;
- tracking and delivery evidence.

Do not store unnecessary personal data solely for dispute defense.

## Public-language rule

Use:
"Under our store policy..."
"for our voluntary exchange/remake process..."
"except where mandatory law provides otherwise..."
"mandatory legal rights remain unaffected."

Avoid:
"Under no circumstances are refunds possible."
"We are never responsible for carrier loss."
"All made-to-order products are legally exempt from returns."
"All tops/bras are hygienic and therefore non-refundable."

Those absolutes may be commercially attractive but can weaken rather than strengthen FEYA's legal/chargeback position when they conflict with mandatory rules.
