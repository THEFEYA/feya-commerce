Owner-sourced product knowledge for writers. These are TRUE design/material stories that give copy concrete, human substance. Use only where the product's confirmed material matches.

## Mirror acrylic / mirror plastic — "liquid metal"
Thermoformed: the acrylic is heat-shaped, which preserves a perfectly smooth surface that looks like liquid metal. The effect is strongest under event lighting and on camera.
Natural contexts (owner-confirmed): red carpet, stage shows and performances, fashion shows, editorial photo shoots, high-profile events (Cannes-style festivals), bloggers/creators. Clubs — occasionally, not the core context. Rave — NOT the style for these pieces; do not pair acrylic corsets/tops with rave framing.
Writer guidance: sell the liquid-metal smoothness and how it reads on camera and under event light; avoid dance-floor/rave framing unless the owner explicitly selects it for a specific product.

## Vegan leather / faux leather (canon)
Durable, glossy mirror-like coating; comfortable against the body; helps pieces keep shape between wears (right-panel canon). One synonym family — never list "vegan leather and faux leather" as two materials.
