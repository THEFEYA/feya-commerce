## Human Owner: business truths

1. Every individual listing is one `canonical_product_id`; a buyer may add several paid variants, separate parts, options, sizes/colours or quantities of that SAME listing at exactly the prices defined in that listing. Do not change the merchandise pricing system.
2. For a cart containing multiple **different** listings, add **EUR 5** for each additional distinct `canonical_product_id` beyond the first: one listing 0, two €5, three €10, four €15, etc. This is a separate **order-level packaging/handling line**; NOT per option, NOT per item quantity, NOT per parcel, NOT a second Standard/Express delivery charge. EUR is now explicitly confirmed. Use server-current immutable merchandise receipts, not client-calculated line counts or amounts.
3. Base shipping stays Standard EUR 19 / Express EUR 35. Owner approved +EUR 20 on an eligible parcel for Australia (AU), Mexico (MX), New Zealand (NZ), but NOT Saudi Arabia solely because of distance. Buyer chooses Standard/Express ONCE at cart level. Product production profiles remain 207/207 with Mon–Fri workdays; saved owner Express currently 6–9 business days, Standard 10–14 days. Event arrival dates are never guaranteed.
4. Eligible export shipping destinations must come from *actual available Ukrposhta OR Nova Poshta shipping from Ukraine*, excluding RU/BY and other blocked/inoperable destinations. Do not make a blank list a universal wildcard or allow countries based solely on ISO membership.

## Primary carrier source snapshots, not a live booking API

**Ukrposhta:** [Published list of export countries/territories, 10 November 2025](https://www.ukrposhta.ua/doc/shipping-outside-ukraine/perelik_krain_pryznachennia_mizhnarodnykh_vidpravlen_10112025.pdf). It contains 233 two-letter published destination codes, with the document's `UK` normalized to ISO `GB`. The full site [World delivery](https://www.ukrposhta.ua/ua/dostavka-po-svitu) describes reach to more than 230 countries/territories. Separate [official no-delivery list updated 12 May 2026](https://e-export.ukrposhta.ua/spysok-krayin-v-yaki-ne-zdijsnyuyetsya-dostavka-stanom-na-15-08-2025/) suspends 22 country destinations including Belarus and Russia. The current source update age means runtime route confirmation still matters.

**Nova Poshta:** [Ukraine outbound service](https://novaposhta.ua/send/international-delivery/how-to-send/) documents Europe direct, international US, Canada and world destinations. [Nova Post international branch destinations](https://novaposhta.ua/international-send?from=ua&to=es) provide confirmed reference for PL/LT/DE/CZ/RO/SK/LV/EE/HU/MD/IT/FR/AT/GB/ES/NL/FI; outbound worldwide PDF [source](https://site-assets.novapost.com/ecf15e88-c596-49d9-9a67-b7fb994f2f09.pdf) has additional routes but is **not** an authenticated real-time rate/country API. We must not confuse inbound NP Shopping import-to-Ukraine countries with shipping FROM Ukraine.

**Express vs Standard:** `Standard` and `Express` are TheFEYA's two buyer-facing commercial shipping methods, not a proof every destination has Ukrposhta EMS or Nova Post Express. Ukrposhta's [EMS service](https://www.ukrposhta.ua/ua/vidpravlennya-ems) reports 130+ supported countries and requires country-by-country checking. The chosen underlying real carrier service and actual route/parcel dimensions must be confirmed before the method is offered in payable checkout.

## Internal candidate policy (not approved shipping)

- `lib/commerceCarrierCountryCandidates.ts` combines the 233 Ukrposhta PDF ISO entries with 19 separately identifiable Nova Post outbound references, removes the 22 temporarily suspended codes, applies owner-denied RU/BY/KP, and holds 4 exceptional regions (AQ/IO/EH/PN) for route-specific review. This produces **209 preliminary country candidates**. Each remains `payable=false`, `standard_verified=false`, `express_verified=false`. In other words, a document-listed country is not automatically an **active customer shipping destination**.
- The suspended list (source dated 2026-05-12) includes AF, BS, BY, BF, BI, HT, GY, GN, GQ, YE, IR, KI, KM, MS, NE, PS, SS, RU, SY, SO, SD, TV. KP is an explicit extra owner/safety exclusion, not falsely attributed to the Ukrposhta suspension article. `UA` is not in the international export allowlist; domestic shipping, if requested, needs separate service-specific pricing.
- Some territories or specific postal codes and the materials/package class will require verification by the chosen carrier. Do not blanket-ban every island and do not blanket-charge remote fees to every island.
- **Next M2 country release gate:** versioned, time-bounded per-carrier/per-country/`standard|express` proof of availability, with verified merchant route and parcel class. A country passes if **at least one** actual provider supports that exact method at the requested destination (and country is not blocked), not merely if present in the published PDF. Postal-address checks may still reject exceptional postcodes. When evidence is unavailable or stale, show `delivery unavailable / request a quote`, not an invented ETA/rate.
- A future trusted provider sync can fill such records automatically and produce a reviewed owner default allowlist. Owner should **not** enter 209 ISO codes manually; do not modify production `served_countries` yet from these documentary candidates because the existing field would falsely signify verified active service to every one of them.
