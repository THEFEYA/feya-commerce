## Purpose and release conditions

Use twelve existing product photographs and approved product copy to test whether Pinterest brings relevant visitors to TheFEYA. Ordinary image Pins can link directly to product pages; this initial manual pilot does not need a retail-catalog integration or a Pinterest advertising tag.

The source is the committed Merchant pilot readback at main `8109709d69ed7366202a9607878134ea7df5f231`, `docs/merchant/pilot-us-20261103/source_live_readback.tsv`. It is newer than the uploaded workbook's provisional HOLD list. Text below is reproduced from that source without product claims being added.

Before publishing: verify the owner's actual brand account, inspect each original photo, open the destination and compare its title/default configuration. Record the real Pin URL and publication timestamp. An unavailable or unsuitable image remains on hold; do not substitute an AI render or an unrelated photograph. Recheck the landing page if publishing after this checkpoint.

Use the existing relevant boards. Possible board themes, only if useful, are Festival Outfit Ideas, Stage & Performance Outfits, and Warrior & Fantasy Costume Ideas. These are suggestions, not existing account or board identities.

## Measurement

Composer input links use utm_source=pinterest, utm_medium=social, utm_campaign=feya_organic_pilot_202611 and a per-product utm_content. Pinterest normalizes the published source/medium to Pinterest/organic; use the observed published destinations for reporting. Keep the bare canonical product URL in site metadata. Do not add prices to Pin text, since stale copied prices would be misleading.

Record Pinterest impressions, saves and outbound clicks where available. In GA4 compare Pinterest visits, product views, add-to-cart and checkout activity; GA4 covers only consenting visitors. Paid customer orders and revenue come from commerce order truth. Owner test visits and the EUR1 connection test are excluded.

Review acquisition after the first published cohort has had time to receive impressions. Do not infer product failure from zero orders while impressions or outbound clicks are negligible. No fabricated conversion target, revenue forecast or guarantee of Google indexing is attached to this draft.
