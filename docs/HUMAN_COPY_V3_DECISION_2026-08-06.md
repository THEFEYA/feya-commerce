# FEYA Human Copy v3 decision — 2026-08-06

## Incident result

The first compact one-pass pilot was cheaper than the retired two-pass flow, but it still failed. The writer copied internal evidence wording (`owner-approved`, `story confirms`, `confirmed`), repeated brand biography, returned the four left blocks in the wrong order, repeated the component checklist in the availability sentence, overused the exact Primary, and produced one repeated `who need` rhythm in Ideal for.

This was a contract failure, not proof that the catalog needs a second automatic writer.

## Production decision

1. Product Truth, keyword roles, block order, What's Included, purchase configuration and validation are code-owned.
2. The language model writes one draft only: intro, About, three or four distinct benefits, four or five audience portraits, final studio close, meta and factual ALT.
3. Writer-visible facts are short neutral `feature -> wearer value` pairs. Provenance remains in the audit layer and cannot enter the prompt as prose.
4. A zero-cost preflight blocks the request when internal provenance or generic independence padding leaks into the brief.
5. Compact positive block frames teach rhythm and section jobs without supplying a finished product paragraph to copy. Mechanical failure classes stay in deterministic tests and validators instead of being paid prompt text.
6. A failed draft is shown as blocked. There is no automatic retry or editor. One targeted repair remains an explicit human action.
7. Human approval is required before save, apply or publish.

## Canonical visible order

1. About this piece
2. What's included (deterministic checklist and one generic availability sentence)
3. Why you'll love it
4. Ideal for
5. Designed for self-expression (the only brand paragraph and the only TheFEYA mention)

## Model and service decision

- Do not add a generic humanizer. A second stochastic rewrite increases factual drift and doubles generation work without fixing Product Truth or layout.
- Do not buy Jasper now. Its Brand Voice, Knowledge, Audience and Style Guide pattern is useful, but API access is a Business-plan feature. FEYA already owns the structured data and approval workflow needed to implement the relevant pattern directly.
- Keep the current single OpenAI writer for the next controlled pilot. The failed pilot is not a fair model comparison because its brief contained the bad wording verbatim.
- If the corrected golden pilot still fails editorial review, run a one-time blind bake-off on five representative approved products through Vercel AI Gateway. Compare one OpenAI, one Anthropic and one Google model with the identical v4 brief. Select one production model by owner preference, deterministic pass rate, edit time and token use. Do not call all models for every catalog item.

## SEO decision

- Product Truth and page-type intent outrank volume.
- Primary exact phrase is an identity signal, not a density target. Use a natural exact or close form in title/H1/meta and a whole-product semantic variation in useful body copy.
- Secondary phrases are vocabulary, not a checklist.
- Keyword repetition is measured only as a stuffing guard.
- Future event, style, persona and component landing pages receive their own intent clusters; broad landing intent is not copied into every PDP.
- Google and AI search use the same people-first foundation. There is no separate LLM keyword-density formula.

## Pilot acceptance gate

- Correct current sellable composition and no unsold item in prose or ALT.
- Correct visible order regardless of model order.
- No internal provenance language.
- No repeated component availability outside What's Included.
- Three or four genuinely different benefits.
- Four or five distinct audience portraits with varied rhythm; cosplay is an original character interpretation.
- TheFEYA appears once, in the final close; `independent` is not used as a selling point.
- Primary passes scope and stuffing checks.
- Structural, commercial and keyword validators contain no blockers.
- Owner reviews the visible preview before any persistence or publication.

## 2026-08-07 pilot amendment

The next one-pass preview proved that cost control worked: one bounded OpenAI request, no retry and no editor. Editorial quality still failed because the prompt contained a full warrior example and the validators left three loopholes:

- four-component sets could repeat their inventory in Meta, Intro and About;
- the exact Primary was allowed a fourth body occurrence;
- robotic phrases such as `clear visual depth`, `body-facing feel`, `for buyers building`, `clear point of view` and `warrior presence` were not named regression classes.

The production contract is therefore:

- exact Primary is owned by SEO title, H1 and meta only; body copy carries a natural whole-product semantic variation;
- component inventory is code-owned for every multi-component product, including large sets;
- the writer receives positive block frames, not the previous complete warrior paragraph;
- cosplay is described positively as an authorial character of the buyer's own, without `copy` or `replica` comparisons;
- the final brand block uses `we/our`, one TheFEYA mention and no generic independence or abstract point-of-view language;
- failed drafts remain visible and blocked; they do not trigger an automatic rewrite.
