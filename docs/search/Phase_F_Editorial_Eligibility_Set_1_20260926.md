## SERP classification and decisions

### 1. Costume measurements / how to measure for a costume

Observed organic results are overwhelmingly measurement guides, forms and educational pages. The two seed SERPs share multiple recurring guide owners and solve the same practical job.

Decision:
**EXISTING_OWNER_UPGRADE → /size-guide**

Reason:
The trust page already exists, already solves the measurement job, and creating a separate blog article would create avoidable cannibalization.

Primary informational cluster:
`costume measurements`

Supporting member:
`how to measure for a costume`

Commercial destinations:
- /collections/bodysuits
- /collections/stage-outfits
- /shop

Index state:
still noindex until the global selective release gate.

### 2. What to wear to Burning Man

Observed results are primarily editorial/practical guidance: first-time burn advice, clothing/costume guidance, community discussion and practical burner fashion articles.

Decision:
**EDITORIAL_GUIDE BUSINESS CASE**

Planned URL:
`/guides/what-to-wear-to-burning-man`

Primary informational cluster:
`what to wear to burning man`

Primary commercial destination:
`/collections/burning-man-looks`

Why separate from the commercial collection:
- commercial `burning man outfits` / `burning man costumes` SERPs are collection-oriented;
- `what to wear to burning man` is informational;
- one collection cannot optimally answer both shopping and practical preparation without becoming bloated.

Current blocker:
Before drafting the guide, retrieve current official Burning Man guidance/rules and distinguish official requirements from TheFEYA styling advice. The guide must not claim event affiliation, safety guarantees or unsupported environmental performance.

### 3. How to clean vegan/faux leather

Demand is high and SERP is informational, but the intent is extremely broad across bags, automotive interiors and generic faux leather.

Decision:
**HOLD — BROAD_MATERIAL_INTENT**

Reason:
TheFEYA has real mirror-coated vegan-leather expertise, but a generic "how to clean vegan leather" article would overreach the site's product scope. Revisit after Q03/Q08 material research or when a narrower costume/material query appears in GSC/Keyword Planner.

The current /care trust page remains the correct customer-help destination.

### 4. How to store costumes

Demand exists, but the observed SERP is noisy and not a stable expert-guide market in the current capture.

Decision:
**HOLD — UNSTABLE/LOW-QUALITY SERP**

The current /care page can cover storage as customer utility without creating another editorial URL.

### 5. Stage / performance guide

Exact guide seeds returned null in this batch. Commercial performance-costume intent is already owned by /collections/stage-outfits.

Decision:
**HOLD FOR Q07 / REAL QUERY EVIDENCE**

Do not create an article simply because competitor research says stage/performance is strategically important.

## Portfolio effect

This editorial review creates only one new planned editorial URL and upgrades one existing trust page:

- /size-guide → informational query owner candidate
- /guides/what-to-wear-to-burning-man → planned editorial business case
- /care → remains trust utility, no generic vegan-leather SEO expansion
- no stage article yet
- no costume-storage article yet

This is consistent with the editorial research principle that a backlog may contain many ideas while only a small number deserve URLs.
