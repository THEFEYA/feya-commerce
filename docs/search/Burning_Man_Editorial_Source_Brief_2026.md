## Facts the guide may state with source attribution

- Burning Man 2026 is held in Black Rock City in the Black Rock Desert.
- Official preparation guidance says participants should read the Survival Guide and prepare for a remote desert environment.
- The official personal checklist includes warm clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses, a particle/dust mask, goggles, rain gear and appropriate footwear among the required/essential preparation items.
- The playa can experience sudden dust storms and whiteouts; official guidance tells participants to use dust masks and goggles during whiteouts.
- Official Leave No Trace guidance says everything brought to the playa can become MOOP and must leave with the participant.
- Official packing guidance advises choosing materials that will not break apart or blow away.
- Official guidance specifically warns against MOOP-prone items such as glitter, loose feathers, rhinestones, sequins, wigs and similar detachable decoration.
- Official 2026 preparation content treats lighting the person/bike at night as a safety issue; fashion styling must not substitute for required visibility/safety gear.
